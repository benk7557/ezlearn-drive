Privacy policy

EZlearn Drive is used only by its owner, on the owner's own server.

  • It uses the Google Drive scope drive.file: it can only see and change files it created itself.
  • It uploads course files to the owner's own Drive. It does not read any other Drive data.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or sent to any third party. The access token stays on the owner's server.
